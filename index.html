Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Second Life Espresso soap actually smell like coffee?

Yes! The soap has a natural coffee scent from the espresso grounds, giving it a rich and refreshing coffee aroma.

Is the espresso texture too rough for sensitive skin?

While the grounds provide a gentle exfoliation, the soap base is crafted to be gentle and support natural cell turnover without irritation.

How long does one bar of Second Life soap typically last?

With normal use, one bar typically lasts several weeks.

Where do you get your espresso grounds?

We collect used espresso grounds from local coffee shops and give them a second life in our soaps.

What is "Conscious Commerce"?

Conscious Commerce means creating products with consideration for people, communities, and the environment.

Risks & Challenges

Our core ingredient repurposed espresso grounds relies on partnerships with local coffee shops.

While we have established strong relationships with several vendors, sudden shifts in their operations could impact our raw material supply.

To mitigate this, we maintain a rotating list of backup local partners to ensure a steady, sustainable stream of biomass.
